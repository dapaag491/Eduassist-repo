### 💡 Flashcards

| Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
| :--- | :--- |
| What is the primary purpose of likes on YouTube? | Likes indicate viewer approval and help YouTube's algorithm determine which content to promote, as they signal high-quality or relevant material to the platform. |
| How do YouTube comments contribute to viewer engagement? | Comments foster direct interaction between creators and viewers, build community, and provide qualitative feedback that helps creators refine their content strategy. |
| Why are shares important for a YouTube video's reach? | Shares amplify content distribution beyond the creator's existing audience, exposing it to new viewers and signaling to the algorithm that the content is valuable enough to spread organically. |
| What role does watch time play in YouTube analytics? | Watch time measures how long viewers engage with a video, which is a critical ranking factor for YouTube's algorithm—videos with higher watch times are more likely to appear in search results and recommendations. |
| How do likes, comments, shares, and watch time collectively influence a video's performance? | These metrics work together to signal content quality and relevance to YouTube's algorithm, with higher engagement levels increasing the likelihood of the video being promoted across the platform. |
| Explain the connection between audience engagement and YouTube's recommendation algorithm. | YouTube prioritizes content that generates strong engagement (likes, comments, shares, watch time) because it suggests the video resonates with viewers, leading to increased visibility and broader reach. |
| Why is fostering audience engagement essential for long-term YouTube success? | Consistent engagement builds loyal subscriber bases, enhances content credibility, and creates feedback loops that inform future content creation while maximizing algorithmic support for sustainable growth. |
