## 🔹 Module 1: Needs & Learner Analysis (ADDIE Analyze)
- **ID:** `node-1`
- **Progress:** [ ] Completed

**Description:**
Conduct audience analysis, assess prior knowledge, and perform task analysis to define learner needs and instructional goals. Lays foundation for all subsequent design decisions.

### 🔗 Resources
- [Analyze Phase of the ADDIE Model: Needs, Learner, and Task Analysis](https://www.instructionaldesign.org/models/addie/) `[article]` - A comprehensive overview of the Analyze phase of ADDIE, covering learner analysis, needs assessment, and task analysis.
- [ADDIE Model: The Analyze Phase](https://youtu.be/PL4yYrIBtYs) `[video]` - A video walkthrough of the Analyze phase, including conducting learner analysis and defining instructional goals.
- [Learner Analysis in Instructional Design](https://www.learningsolutionsmag.com/articles/learner-analysis-what-instructional-designers-need-to-know) `[article]` - Detailed article on how to perform a learner analysis, including assessing prior knowledge and learner characteristics.
- [Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning (Mayer's 12 Principles)](https://www.celt.iastate.edu/teaching/teaching-format/online-and-blended-learning/cognitive-theory-of-multimedia-learning/) `[documentation]` - Official resource explaining the Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning, relevant to how learner analysis informs multimedia design.
- [Task Analysis in Instructional Design](https://www.digitallearninginstitute.com/blog/task-analysis-instructional-design) `[article]` - Guide to performing task analysis to identify what learners need to know and be able to do.
