### 🔗 Resources
- [Cryptography Basics for CTFs (Hacker101)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6vq_YH6h5I) `[video]` - An introductory video covering fundamental cryptography concepts including encoding, hashing, symmetric/asymmetric encryption, and common CTF challenges related to these topics.
- [Crypto 101: Free Online Cryptography Book](https://www.crypto101.io/) `[article]` - A comprehensive free online book explaining core cryptographic principles, including encoding schemes, hash functions, and encryption algorithms with practical examples.
- [OWASP Cryptographic Storage Cheat Sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Cryptographic_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html) `[documentation]` - Documentation outlining best practices for cryptographic storage in applications, covering algorithm selection, key management, and common pitfalls in encryption implementation.
- [OverTheWire: Krypton Wargames Walkthrough](https://overthewire.org/wargames/krypton/) `[article]` - Interactive walkthrough of cryptographic challenges involving encoding, XOR ciphers, and basic hashing, designed to reinforce theoretical knowledge through hands-on practice.
- [RSA Encryption Explained Simply (Computerphile)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z5q8RZQGzqY) `[video]` - A clear explanation of how RSA asymmetric encryption works, which is crucial for understanding advanced cryptography concepts often encountered in CTF challenges.
